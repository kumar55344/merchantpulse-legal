MerchantPulse Account Deletion

How to request account deletion

Request account deletion

You can delete your MerchantPulse account using the Delete Account option in the app's Profile section. If you cannot access the app, you may email a deletion request to our support address.

Email a deletion request

Email: yourtimes555@gmail.com

In your request, provide the email address associated with your MerchantPulse account. Do not send your password, OTP, UPI PIN or banking credentials.

What happens after a verified request?

  • We will verify that the request relates to the account owner before taking action.
  • The associated Firebase Authentication account and merchant profile document are intended to be deleted when the deletion operation succeeds.
  • If we cannot verify ownership or need further information, we may contact you using the information supplied with the request.

An email request does not itself delete an account. It must be received, verified and processed.

Local data and device records

When you delete your account using the in-app Delete Account feature, the app's multi-step deletion coordinator permanently removes all local SQLite transaction records, saved QR profiles, and account-owned QR image files associated with that account from your device.

Older unassigned legacy records and records belonging to other accounts are protected and are not deleted by this operation.

Existing encrypted backup files stored in your personal Google Drive account are NOT automatically deleted by this flow. They remain in your private Google Drive Application Data folder until you choose to delete them directly through Google Drive settings.

If an account deletion step encounters a network error or interruption midway, the app retains a local recovery journal so the operation can be resumed or retried safely on the next launch rather than leaving partial data in an inconsistent state.

Processing and support

We will review verified requests and process them as soon as reasonably possible, subject to applicable legal obligations. If a request cannot be completed, we will explain the reason where appropriate.

For questions, contact yourtimes555@gmail.com.
